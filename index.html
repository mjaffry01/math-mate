Math Mate: 200 practice questions

Questions 1 to 200 from the Math Mate workbook (pages 38 to 53). Choose 1, 2, 3 … to go through them in book order, or By topic to practise one kind of question at a time (16 topics). Each question keeps its book number, and the small “p.” under it is the book page.

Try it yourself first. Tap the option you think is right. If it is wrong, a 💡 Hint appears, so have another go. Wrong again? The full solution opens and you can learn how to do it.

Stuck before you answer? Tap 💡 Hint. After the hint you can also open the full solution: what is given, what to find, the rule, the classroom method with every calculation, and a check.

Your results are saved on this device: ⭐ right on the first try, 💡 right after a hint, 📖 needed the solution.

Many solutions include a Picture it diagram (bar models, clocks, maps, shapes) so you can see the idea before the numbers.

Every solution also has an ⚡ Exam shortcut: the quick trick to get the answer fast in the exam.

Questions marked “Check” need to be confirmed against the book. Questions marked “Blurry” could not be read reliably from the photo.
